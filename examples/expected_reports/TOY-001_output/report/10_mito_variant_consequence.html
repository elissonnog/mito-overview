This page assigns a local biological consequence class to mitochondrial alternate-allele candidate sites. Protein-coding sites are annotated against mitochondrial CDS intervals and the vertebrate mitochondrial genetic code, while tRNA, rRNA, control-region, and intergenic sites are classified by feature context. The annotation table retains one row per variant-feature consequence; site counts deduplicate genomic positions, variant counts deduplicate position/ref/alt keys, and row counts retain overlapping annotations. ClinVar annotations are shown only when an exact local position/ref/alt match is present in the configured mitochondrial ClinVar callset.
