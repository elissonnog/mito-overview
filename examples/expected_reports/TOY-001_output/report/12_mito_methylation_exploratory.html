Sample: TOY-001 | Region: MT:whole_mito

This page provides an exploratory mitochondrial methylation summary using real mitochondrial bedmethyl rows from both the phased and no-phased workflows. The phased tracks are shown separately, a phased-derived all-read proxy is reconstructed from the phased tracks, and that proxy is compared against the real no-phased all-read mitochondrial bedmethyl track. This page is intended for pattern-finding and QC context rather than strong biological claims about mtDNA methylation.
