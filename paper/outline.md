# Preprint Outline

## Working paper concept
A modular ONT mtDNA interpretation and reporting framework with long-read-aware QC and optional interoperability with established mtDNA tools.

## Core sections
1. motivation and fragmentation of current mtDNA analysis workflows
2. software architecture
3. analytical layers and biological rationale
4. validation on representative ONT mtDNA samples
5. interoperability with optional external mtDNA tools
6. discussion of reproducibility, portability, and translational utility
